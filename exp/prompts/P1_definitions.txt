Question: At the evaluation time, what is the status of the target request?
Choose exactly one label: open, canceled, fulfilled, full.

Label definitions (the "initiator" is the actor who wrote the target request):
- open: the initiator has not closed the request. Replies from other people alone do not close it. A change to a different, unrelated plan does not count.
- canceled: the initiator says the plan will not happen as proposed, or is no longer needed, without it having been secured.
- fulfilled: the initiator confirms the goal is secured, e.g. a ride, companion or item is found, or a specific person's offer is accepted. It does not need to have physically happened yet.
- full: the target request is an offer with a limited number of seats, items or spots, and the initiator confirms all of them are taken.
