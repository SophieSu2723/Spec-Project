You will read a group chat. Each line is one dialogue entry, written as:
<entry ID> <actor>: <text>
If a line ends with [reply m-XX], that entry replies to entry m-XX.

Here are four solved examples.

Example 1
Target request: m-02
m-01  kate: Is the printer broken again?

m-03  lily: Count me in.   [reply m-02]
m-04  tom: Me too.   [reply m-02]
m-05  ryan: Both spots are taken, the study room is full.   [reply m-02]
STATUS: full
